# My agent: greenhouse-agent
One-liner: A conversational plant care and greenhouse assistant that helps plant lovers track plant care schedules, manage their plant collection, and order supplies with a catalog of greenhouse plants and care products.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Plant owner preferences, skill level, home lighting conditions, and watering history
- Tools: Look up plant care requirements, log watering and fertilizing events, search greenhouse plant inventory
- Catalog/UI: A catalog of greenhouse plants, species info, and care supplies rendered as cards and tables
- Image gen: Visual care guides, ideal lighting/placement illustrations, and healthy growth previews
- Sandbox: Calculate watering schedules based on temperature/humidity and fertilizer dilution ratios

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): Code sandbox for care schedule calculations, Cloud Storage for plant photos, video with Omni
